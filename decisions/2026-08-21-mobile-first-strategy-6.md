# Decision — Affiliate transparency model

**Date:** 2026-08-21 · **Status:** accepted (mentor sync)
**Source:** `../meetings/2026-08-21-mobile-first-strategy.md` (~01:01:50–01:04:15)

## Context
Affiliate links (hotels, restaurants) are the natural monetization for the recommendation flow, but biasing core recommendations toward paying partners would break user trust — and judges will ask about the revenue model.

## Decision
Keep **core recommendations neutral** (review/data-driven). Affiliate/partner content lives in a clearly separated **"special offers / community deals"** section presented as a bonus ("this place currently has an offer"), never silently promoted into the main ranking. Pitch framing: "opening opportunities for local providers affiliated with the platform, visible as offers — recommendations stay neutral."

## Why
Answers the monetization question with a model that survives ethical scrutiny from both judges and users.

## Consequences
- UI needs an offers/deals surface distinct from recommendation results (post-PoC).
- Community/travel-buddy forum ideas noted as future options — explicitly not in current scope.
