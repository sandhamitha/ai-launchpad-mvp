# Decision — Differentiate on SL-local data + autonomous agents

**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Status:** accepted
**Source:** Planning session `../meetings/2026-07-27-agentic-companion-planning.md`

## Context
Global players (Google, Gemini) and generalist AI travel tools exist. We need a defensible edge that judges and users immediately understand.

## Decision
Position on the **intersection of deep Sri-Lanka-specific local knowledge + advanced autonomous-agent flows with continuous real-time assistance** — a combination global competitors don't offer.

## Why
- Generalists (Gemini/Google) automate basic tasks but won't ship a personalized, always-on travel companion for SL soon.
- Competitors have **regressed** — they stopped asking follow-up planning questions → opening for better interaction + accuracy.
- SL-specific data (emergencies, local nuance) is the moat generalists can't easily replicate.

## Consequences / trade-offs
- Must match competitors' core features **and** add the agentic + local-data layer — parity plus differentiation.
- Depends on assembling quality SL-specific knowledge (RAG corpus) — sourcing/curation is on the critical path.
